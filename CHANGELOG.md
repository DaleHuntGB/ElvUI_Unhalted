# V1.12

## New for WoW Forever

- **Navigation:** an arrow with the distance to your tracked quest or map pin.
  - Shows your faction's flight paths on the world map, with unlearned ones faded.
  - Counts down to landing while you're on a flight.
- **QuestieDB features:** these need the QuestieDB addon.
  - The Navigation arrow points at the nearest mob, object or NPC for your next objective, then at the turn-in NPC when the quest is done.
  - **Available Quests:** marks quest givers on the world map with the quests you can accept.
  - **Quest Objectives:** marks where to kill, loot, talk to or use things for your tracked quests, including the patrol routes of wandering targets.
  - **Level Zones:** borders each area of a zone map and shows its creature level range.
- **Dungeon Journal:** Forever's dungeons with their bosses and loot, with a key binding to open it.
- **World Map Zone Levels:** shows each zone's level range under its name.
- **Objective Tracker sorting:** orders tracked quests by level or proximity, with completed quests last as an option.
- **Auto Purchase:** tops up vendor items to a set quantity.
- **Hunter:**
  - **Ammo Counter.**
  - **Feed Pet:** feeds your pet the best food in your bags with `/click FeedPet`.
  - **Beast Lore:** beast tooltips show which pet abilities they teach.
  - **Merge Icons:** merges the icons of abilities that share a cooldown.

## New for all versions

- **Remove Fog of War:** reveals unexplored areas on the world map, desaturated.
- **Coloured Quest Mobs:** colours the nameplates of mobs that are part of an active quest.
- **Focus Texture:** overlays a texture on your focus target's nameplate.
- **Nameplate Distance** slider under CVars. The Camera Distance slider now goes further.
- **Totem Tracker** mover.

## Changes

- Auto Quest skips low-level (grey) quests and no longer completes turn-ins that cost gold.
- Retail-only features no longer load on Forever.
- Reorganised options.
- Updated profiles.
- Performance improvements.
